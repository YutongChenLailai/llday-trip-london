    A TINY FIELD GUIDE

    Your hand.Your next adventure.

    Allow your camera and sit where your face and hand are visible. Your face appears in a clean oval with a dashed edge. Your hands are cut out at the wrist; hair, neck, torso and arms stay outside.

    Pinch your thumb and index finger to hold the 3D kunai. Flick forward or upward and open your fingers to draw a random next stop. Consecutive throws never pick the same place.

    Keep your hand in frame. Good light and a plain background help. You can also use mouse mode: click anywhere on the map, or press Space to throw at the crosshair.

    Camera frames are processed in your browser and never uploaded. The regional map is bundled with this page. These are day-trip ideas, not live train schedules: check your travel date and last return train. Visited places are saved in this browser and excluded from future throws; untick a place to include it again.
